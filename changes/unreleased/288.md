kind: feature

`close-task <pr> [--comment file]` closes an unmerged task PR without merging,
stopping its recorded worker like merge follow-up (a PR with no worker record
skips silently). It refuses PRs outside the configured repository, non-task PRs
and closed PRs before any write, posts the optional checked closing comment, and
leaves card, worktree and linked-issue follow-up to the watcher.
