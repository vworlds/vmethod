The `merge-task` follow-up no longer reports a false `worker stop failed` for a PR that never had a vmethod worker, and its worktree cleanup is no longer blocked as a live or unknown session. With no worker record the follow-up treats the worker as absent, not unknown: it skips the stop silently, needs no session close and removes the worktree when the safety guards are met. A PR whose worker record exists but whose session cannot be resolved keeps today's cautious reporting and cleanup skip.
