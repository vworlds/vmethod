kind: feature

`vmethod watch` appends one short reminder line to the end of a delivery's output, at most once every 30 minutes: `REMINDER (owner rule): chat = #n pointers only or warnings; discuss on GitHub. Don't explain here what you will put in an issue/PR. Evidence, plans and summaries go on the issue/PR, never in chat.` Empty deliveries stay silent so the line never stands alone, the interval persists in the watcher state directory across restarts, and the reminder is delivery metadata rather than an event: recordings, replay and acknowledgements hold events only.
