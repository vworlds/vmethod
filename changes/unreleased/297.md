kind: feature

The two worker management verbs now handle a recorded session whose worktree is
gone like the other stopped reasons (#286): `worker start <pr>` restarts the task
without `--replace` and without an interrupt or idle wait, carrying the record's
segment history and usage into the fresh worktree `prepare` creates on the PR
branch, and `worker stop <pr>` reads the status first, skips the interrupt POST,
clears `pendingConflict` and returns `#n already stopped (worktree gone)` instead
of failing on the raw opencode 404.
